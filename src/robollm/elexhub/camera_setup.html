Print and connect

Open the calibration sheets ↗. Print at 100% scale, check the 100 mm reference line, and keep the ChArUco board flat.

Start with index 0. If it cannot detect your webcam, check the connection or try another index. Starting again creates a new session and keeps earlier files.

2

Capture different views

Aim for 20 views, with at least 10 detected corners in each. Hold the board still for each photo.

  • Move the board across the center and edges of the image.
  • Tilt it left, right, up and down; vary the distance.
  • Keep focus and zoom fixed for calibration and later use.
