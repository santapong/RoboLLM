# Model provenance

The demonstration downloads the Unitree G1 and H1 descriptions from Google
DeepMind's MuJoCo Menagerie:
[G1](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/8161bba264d7fa7c99ca301e91e7fb44737676ad/unitree_g1) and
[H1](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie/tree/8161bba264d7fa7c99ca301e91e7fb44737676ad/unitree_h1),
revision `8161bba264d7fa7c99ca301e91e7fb44737676ad` (retrieved 2026-09-09).
Menagerie's G1 README identifies the source as Unitree's public MJCF description.
Its H1 README states that Unitree directly provided the original URDF and assets.
Both models and their meshes have a BSD-3-Clause license; `fetch_model.py` preserves
the upstream LICENSE and README beside the downloaded model. It verifies Git
blob hashes against the pinned revision and records SHA256 hashes in
`assets/unitree_g1/provenance.json` and `assets/unitree_h1/provenance.json`.
Assets are ignored by Git. G1 requires 38 files / 19,693,625 bytes; H1 requires
24 files / 14,339,575 bytes. Fetch only the desired robot with
`python fetch_model.py --model g1` or `--model h1`; construction never downloads.

The demo modifies the model **in memory**: removes the floating base, mounts
the pelvis on a visible stand, adds non-colliding hand sites and target marker,
and adds a floor, lighting, and camera. Original joint ranges, inertias,
collision meshes and force limits are retained. Position targets are limited
to joint ranges and rate-limited. Motion is generated by damped least-squares
inverse kinematics and position servos, with MuJoCo integrating actual joint
dynamics under gravity. H1 originally uses torque motors: this experiment
replaces them in memory with position servos (`kp=500`, critical damping ratio),
retaining each original torque ceiling as the actuator force range. H1 has
19 joints and four-joint arms, so its wave animates shoulder yaw; G1 has
29 joints and seven-joint arms, including wrists. Workspaces and camera views
are specific to each robot; a workspace box does not guarantee reachability.

## Original concept models

`models/<id>/model.json` and `model.xml` register a local original concept.
These must declare `source_kind: "original-procedural"`; custom models cannot
replace official model IDs. Original concept geometry, articulation, mass,
inertia and joint limits are design estimates, and have no Unitree provenance.
Image-guided procedural modeling is distinct from calibrated multi-view
reconstruction or measured hardware identification.

The XML supplies the robot, assets/materials, a top-level fixed or free pelvis,
finite limited hinge joints with position servos, and G1-style arm joint names.
Each arm needs `*_hand_tip` or `*_wrist_yaw_link`; provided hand-tip sites are
preserved. The decorator adds the floor, stand, target and lights, removes any
free pelvis joint, and derives support height from pelvis position. Joint
names, controller actuation and ranges are validated before stepping.

Required metadata: `id`, `label`, `source_kind`, `source_revision`, `description`,
and per-arm `workspace` with `min`, `max`, `default` XYZ metre vectors.
Optional metadata: `home` (joint-name to radians), `reference_image` (relative
path), `view_center`, and `view_distance`. Use
`HumanoidSimulation(model_name="<id>")`, or direct
`custom_model_path`/`custom_metadata` arguments for a standalone local model.
The reported `model_sha256` identifies the exact decorated scene XML, including
render settings and absolute asset paths; it is not a claim of measured physics.

The supported-base experiment does not demonstrate
walking, balancing, learned policies, VLM inference, WAM inference, grasping,
or physical robot operation. No ROS installation is required.
